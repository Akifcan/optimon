## What is Optimon?

Optimon is an AI-powered vault on Monad that automatically distributes your crypto across yield strategies. Our AI agent **Hoot** continuously monitors performance and rebalances your portfolio in real-time to maximize returns, like a smart savings account that optimizes itself.

Just deposit and let Hoot handle the rest. No manual rebalancing. No DeFi expertise needed.

## Why should you use it?

- **Set and forget:** Deposit your MON and walk away. Hoot monitors strategies 24/7 and rebalances when needed.
- **AI-optimized returns** — Hoot analyzes APY, risk scores, and market conditions to find the best allocation for your funds.
- **Risk management built in:** Choose your risk tolerance (Conservative / Balanced / Aggressive) and Hoot adapts accordingly.
- **Full transparency:**  See exactly where your money is, how much you're earning, and why Hoot made each decision.
- **Chat with your agent:** Ask Hoot anything about your portfolio. It explains in plain language.

## How it works

```
You deposit MON
    |
Optimon Vault splits it across strategies
    |
Lending (earn interest) + LP Pool (earn trading fees) + Staking (earn rewards)
    |
Hoot monitors performance and rebalances automatically
    |
You earn optimized returns
```

## Future Plans...

Optimon is currently a working prototype on Monad Testnet. Here's where we're headed:

- **Real DeFi integrations:** Connect to live Monad protocols (lending markets, DEX liquidity pools, staking validators) instead of mock strategies
- **Multi-token support:** Accept any token, not just MON. Auto-swap and route into the best strategies
- **Smarter Hoot:** On-chain data feeds, historical performance tracking, and predictive rebalancing based on market trends
- **Social & competitive:** Leaderboards, vault performance sharing, and community-driven strategy suggestions
- **Gasless experience:** Meta-transactions so users never think about gas fees
- **Mobile app:** Deposit, track, and chat with Hoot from your phone

Our vision: **make earning yield as easy as putting money in a savings account** — but with DeFi-level returns, powered by AI, on Monad's high-performance chain.
